[**Dengue transmission and intervention modelling**]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link _research_page/dengue_modelling.md %}){:target="_blank"} 
Understanding dengue transmission dynamics and evaluating vaccination, Wolbachia-based vector control, and other intervention strategies across different epidemiological settings.


[**Mathematical & Economic Modelling for Vaccination and Immunisation Evaluation (MEMVIE):**]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link _activeresearch/MEMVIE.md %}){:target="_blank"} Epidemiological modelling and health economic modelling to underpin vaccination policy for the Department of Health.


[**Spatial transmission dynamics and human mobility**]

Investigating how spatial heterogeneity, population connectivity, and human mobility influence disease transmission and the effectiveness of control measures.

[**Optimising public health interventions and resources**]

Developing modelling and optimisation approaches to identify effective intervention strategies and inform the allocation of limited public health resources.
